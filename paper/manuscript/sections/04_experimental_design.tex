\section{Experimental Design}

\subsection{Two Evaluation Scopes and Their Metrics}

Forecast metrics use all overlapping rolling test forecasts. Precision is the fraction of intervals predicted Empty that are later labelled Empty, while recall is the fraction of camera-labelled Empty intervals recovered by the model. Changing the score threshold trades precision against recall. The primary metric, Empty-class area under the precision--recall curve (AUPRC), summarizes that trade-off across thresholds; larger values indicate better discrimination. AUPRC is informative to report alongside other measures when the two classes have unequal frequencies~\cite{SaitoRehmsmeier2015}. Empty precision, recall, and F1 in the model-comparison table use a descriptive $p_{\mathrm{Empty}}\geq0.5$ cutoff. The operating policy uses the separately selected threshold in Eq.~\eqref{eq:conflict}.

Policy metrics use a different, non-overlapping scope: 39 validation and 43 test midnight-labelled 96-step horizons. The latter have an effective 00:15 issue boundary under the completed-bin convention in Section~III. For every model, the reported threshold comes from the validation policy horizons. The fixed test policy reports recommended, camera-label-safe, and conflict intervals; stable recommended and camera-label-safe windows; coverage; and Eq.~\eqref{eq:opportunity}. The temporal split and fixed-policy evaluation preserve time order when estimating time-series performance~\cite{Cerqueira2020}.

\subsection{Uncertainty, Diagnostics, and Reproducibility}

Uncertainty is quantified with 2,000 paired daily-block bootstrap resamples of the 43 test policy horizons while holding model weights and thresholds fixed. The resulting intervals are conditional on the selected model and policy; selection uncertainty lies outside them. Brier score, log loss, and 10-bin quantile expected calibration error are reported as held-out diagnostics of the uncalibrated scores. A sensitivity analysis varies the minimum window length from 15 min to 4 h while preserving the validation-selected threshold.

The saved-output path validates aligned prediction exports, repeats the validation-only blend and threshold selection, and regenerates the canonical result artifacts from stored scores. End-to-end model retraining follows a separate workflow. Repository tests check split separation, timestamp offsets, convex-weight selection, policy accounting, and the requirement that the two exploratory selection routines freeze candidates before reading their test diagnostics.

\subsection{Primary and Exploratory Analyses}

The canonical analysis is the validation-selected primary hybrid in Eq.~\eqref{eq:blend}, with the same protocol applied to fixed reference models. The repository also contains a test-ranked balanced hybrid, an expanded joint weight-threshold search, and a window-aware search. Appendix~A classifies these enlarged-search candidates as exploratory because their historical test diagnostics come from a previously inspected period.
