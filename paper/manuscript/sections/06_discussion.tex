\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Interpretation}

The historical average's performance shows that a stable schedule can provide a highly competitive Empty forecast in a single office building. The hybrid's small point AUPRC advantage falls within the paired daily-block uncertainty intervals, so its value rests on the combined trade-off. In the saved test period, its selected operating point has approximately the same processed load-proxy opportunity as LightGBM and zero camera-labelled occupied recommendations. Forecast quality, conflict rate, and opportunity together provide a more informative comparison than forecast rank alone.

The offline opportunity formulation makes the recommendation rule, duration requirement, conflict denominator, and load proxy inspectable. The stable-window rule removes short isolated intervals. Equation~\eqref{eq:opportunity} records the processed load coinciding with the remaining recommendations. Translating that overlap into operational benefit requires a counterfactual and constraints on equipment, indoor conditions, tariffs, occupants, and later load effects. Those additions would allow future work to estimate energy savings, flexibility capacity, carbon effects, and comfort outcomes.

\subsection{Validity Limits}

The temporal evidence supports an effective 00:15 post-bin boundary: a stored 00:00 anchor contains the completed $[00{:}00,00{:}15)$ input bin and targets $[00{:}15,\text{next }00{:}15)$. A prospective timing claim requires original source streams, observation-end timestamps, and source-side imputation lineage. The repository begins with a cleaned release whose documented upstream processing can include non-local imputation. Its post-import row-order fill establishes internal ordering from that point forward; source-causal verification remains a requirement for the prospective rerun.

The data cover selected south zones in one building and a 43-horizon test policy period. Cross-building validation is therefore the next test of generalization. Missed camera detections would label occupied intervals as Empty, so ``camera-label-safe'' and ``conflict-free'' describe agreement with the recorded label. Wi-Fi has substantial pre-fill missingness, and its forward fill uses unlimited staleness. The HVAC and lighting streams used in $q$ are each 8.03\% missing before row-order filling. Alternative sensor-availability and load-imputation patterns deserve sensitivity analysis. A solar-pattern diagnostic provides the source-timezone interpretation because the documentation leaves it unspecified.

The bootstrap conditions on selected weights and thresholds. Resampling all-zero conflict blocks yields all-zero conflict samples. Selection uncertainty is important because the nominal blend winner exceeds the runner-up by 0.000032 validation AUPRC and the validation-only perturbations in Table~\ref{tab:selection-stability} favor several nearby candidates. Deep-model construction also preceded seed reset, and the saved exports contain averaged scores in place of aligned per-seed predictions. These artifacts support post-bin validation-before-test selection and limit seed-dispersion analysis. A confirmatory evaluation would preregister the protocol, control model initialization, and reserve a fresh test firewall.

\subsection{Status of Expanded Searches and Next Evidence}

The repository's decision-aware joint search and window-aware search enlarge the validation search surface, and their historical test diagnostics come from a previously inspected period. Appendix~A therefore treats their point estimates as exploratory and identifies the window-aware candidate as a frozen future challenger. Decision-focused learning would additionally require downstream-objective training~\cite{Wilder2019,ElmachtoubGrigas2022}.

The next empirical experiment should obtain source streams with observation-end timestamps and imputation lineage, declare a bin-end/issue-time convention, initialize seeds before model construction, lock package versions, retrain under the frozen protocol, and evaluate a later untouched period or a new building. A simulator or intervention with explicit comfort and equipment constraints can then test control and energy outcomes. This sequence addresses the operational questions examined in occupancy-aware HVAC and ventilation studies~\cite{Peng2018,Zhuang2022,Wietzke2024}.
