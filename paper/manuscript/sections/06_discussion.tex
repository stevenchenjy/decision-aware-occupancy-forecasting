\section{Discussion}

\subsection{What the Comparison Does and Does Not Show}

The historical average's performance is informative rather than an inconvenience to be optimized away. A stable schedule can produce a highly competitive Empty forecast in a single office building, and the small point AUPRC advantage of the primary hybrid is not decisive under the reported paired daily-block intervals. The hybrid's practical distinction in this saved test period is not a large forecast-score gain: it selects an operating point with approximately the same processed load-proxy opportunity as LightGBM and no camera-labeled occupied recommendations. This is a reason to report forecast and policy metrics together, not a reason to declare a universally superior model.

The offline opportunity formulation also provides a useful guardrail for interpretation. It makes the recommendation rule, duration requirement, conflict denominator, and load proxy inspectable. In particular, it avoids calling every correctly predicted Empty interval operationally useful. A short isolated interval is removed by the stable-window rule. At the same time, Eq.~\eqref{eq:opportunity} has no counterfactual. The processed HVAC and lighting values may not be controllable at that moment, shifting a load may create a later penalty, and a real intervention would require constraints on equipment, indoor conditions, tariffs, and occupants. This paper therefore does not estimate savings, flexibility capacity, carbon reduction, or comfort preservation.

\subsection{Validity Limits}

The temporal and provenance limitation is fundamental. A stored 00:00 anchor contains a completed $[00{:}00,00{:}15)$ input bin, so the current evidence supports only an effective 00:15 post-bin boundary and targets $[00{:}15,\text{next }00{:}15)$. It does not establish a midnight-issued or real-time recommendation. Moreover, the repository starts from the cleaned release; its documented upstream cleaning can include non-local imputation, and neither original raw streams nor observation-end timestamps are committed. Row-order forward fill performed after import cannot repair that provenance gap. Thus, the saved output is internally ordered but cannot be called fully prospective or source-causally leakage-free.

Several additional limitations constrain the result. The data cover selected south zones in one building and a 43-horizon test policy period; external and cross-building validation are unavailable. The camera-count label can miss occupants and does not establish physical absence. Results called camera-label-safe or conflict-free apply only to that label. Wi-Fi has substantial pre-fill missingness without an age cap, and the HVAC and lighting streams used in $q$ are each 8.03\% missing before row-order filling. The study does not establish robustness to alternative sensor availability or load-imputation patterns. The source timezone is inferred through a solar-pattern diagnostic rather than explicitly documented.

The statistical limitations are also material. The bootstrap conditions on selected weights and thresholds and cannot generate unseen conflicts when all resampled blocks have zero conflicts. The nominal blend winner exceeds the runner-up by only 0.000032 validation AUPRC, and the validation-only perturbation diagnostics in Table~\ref{tab:selection-stability} demonstrate selection instability. Deep-model seed labels do not control initialization because construction preceded seed reset. Finally, saved exports do not contain aligned per-seed predictions or all raw source files, so seed dispersion and end-to-end retraining cannot be reconstructed. The available artifacts verify a post-bin validation-before-test selection procedure for the canonical saved-output analysis, but they are not a preregistered, fully blinded confirmatory evaluation with a fresh test firewall.

\subsection{Status of Expanded Searches and Next Evidence}

The repository's decision-aware joint search and window-aware search are not evidence for replacing the canonical policy. Their code now freezes validation candidates before reading the test diagnostic, but both enlarge the search surface and their historical test diagnostics are not fresh untouched confirmation. Appendix~A labels their point estimates as exploratory or as frozen future challengers rather than performance improvements. This distinction is essential because decision-focused learning would require downstream-objective training, which this pipeline does not perform~\cite{Wilder2019,ElmachtoubGrigas2022}.

The next defensible empirical experiment is more specific than simply rerunning the saved script: obtain source streams with observation-end timestamps and imputation lineage; declare a bin-end/issue-time convention; correct seed initialization before model construction; lock package versions; retrain without consulting future test outcomes; and apply the frozen policy to a later untouched period or a new building. A simulator or intervention with explicit comfort and equipment constraints is then needed to test actual control and energy claims. That sequence would make it possible to evaluate the operational questions addressed by occupancy-aware HVAC and ventilation studies~\cite{Peng2018,Zhuang2022,Wietzke2024}.
