\section{Conclusion}

This paper presents a transparent offline, post-bin assessment from saved next-24-h occupancy scores to stable Empty-window recommendations. In one Building~59 case study, a validation-selected historical--LightGBM--compact-Transformer score blend achieved 0.8514 all-overlap Empty AUPRC across 388,032 test rows and, at its fixed one-hour policy, 490.1~kWh of offline safe load opportunity with zero observed camera-label conflicts across 259 recommended test intervals. The historical schedule baseline remained nearly equal in AUPRC, and the primary hybrid's paired differences from LightGBM and the schedule baseline were not decisive for forecast quality or for opportunity versus LightGBM. Validation perturbations further show that the selected blend and threshold should not be treated as uniquely optimal.

The contribution is therefore a reproducible saved-output forecast-to-policy assessment with explicit evidence boundaries. It does not demonstrate energy savings, comfort preservation, physical absence, controller safety, calibrated risk, real-time availability, or generalization. The left-labelled bin timing and cleaned-source provenance mean that an operational claim requires an empirical rerun with raw/provenance-tagged streams, corrected deep-model seed initialization, a locked protocol, and a later untouched evaluation period before submission as a prospective operational method.
