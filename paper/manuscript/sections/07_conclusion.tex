\section{Conclusion}

This paper presents an offline, post-bin assessment from saved next-24-h occupancy scores to stable Empty-window recommendations. The primary hybrid combines a historical schedule, LightGBM, and a compact Transformer so that recurring, nonlinear, and sequential patterns can contribute. In one Building~59 case study, the validation-selected blend achieved 0.8514 all-overlap Empty AUPRC across 388,032 test rows. Its fixed one-hour policy identified 490.1~kWh of offline safe load opportunity with zero observed camera-label conflicts across 259 recommended test intervals. This operating point offers nearly the opportunity of LightGBM with fewer observed conflicts and much more opportunity than the conflict-free historical policy. The historical baseline's nearly equal AUPRC, paired intervals that cross zero, and validation perturbations identify a competitive set of blends.

The contribution is a reproducible forecast-to-policy assessment that connects discrimination, stable-window conflict, and load opportunity. A prospective evaluation with provenance-tagged source streams, corrected deep-model initialization, a locked protocol, and a later untouched period can extend this evidence to real-time operation and measured control outcomes.
